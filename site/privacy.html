Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 November 2026

PDF Desk does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data.

There is no account, no telemetry, no analytics, no advertising, and no crash reporting service. Nothing you open in PDF Desk leaves your computer.

What PDF Desk stores, and where

PDF Desk saves a small amount of state locally so it can pick up where you left off. Settings and reading history are stored in Documents\PDFDesk\PDFDesk.json. The default bookshelf is under Documents\PDFDesk\Library and can be changed in Settings. Thumbnails, signatures, clipboard copies and error logs are under %LOCALAPPDATA%\PDFDesk.

WhatWhy
Paths of recently-opened files, and their page-1 thumbnailsTo show the recent-documents list
Per-document reading position, zoom and rotationTo reopen a document where you stopped
Bookmarks you createTo restore them next time you open that document
Your preferences (theme, sidebar, keyboard options)To remember your settings
Signatures you draw or import, in signatures\So you can reuse one instead of redrawing it
A copy of a PDF with unsaved edits that you copied to the clipboard, in share\To include your edits when you paste the file
An error log, if something goes wrongTo help diagnose a crash

When you copy a PDF with unsaved edits, PDF Desk attempts to remove copies in share\ that are more than a day old. You can delete the folder at any time.

Your signature never leaves the device. It is stored as an ordinary image file in %LOCALAPPDATA%\PDFDesk\signatures, is never uploaded or transmitted, and is only written into a PDF when you place it on a page yourself. You can delete any saved signature from the Sign tool, or by deleting that folder.

When you import a photo of your signature, PDF Desk removes the paper background itself, on your machine. No online service, no AI model and no upload is involved: the photo is read, processed and saved without ever leaving PDF Desk.

To clear local application data, delete the files you no longer want in these folders. The library folder can contain your own PDF copies.

Network access

PDF Desk makes no network requests at all.

Opening a web link hands it to your browser.

Your documents

PDF Desk opens PDF files from your device and renders them locally. Documents are never uploaded, indexed, or transmitted anywhere. Annotations, filled form fields and page edits are written back only to the file you choose to save, on your own device.

Form data stays in the document. PDF Desk fills PDF forms locally and never submits them anywhere. A PDF's own Submit button is not wired up: PDF Desk does not execute form actions or form JavaScript, so nothing you type into a form can be sent over the network by the document itself.

Copy PDF File puts the PDF on the Windows clipboard when you choose the command. For a document with unsaved edits, PDF Desk creates a local copy that includes your edits. You choose where to paste the file.

Children

PDF Desk is a document viewer suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes

Any change to this policy will be committed to the repository, so the history is public and auditable.
